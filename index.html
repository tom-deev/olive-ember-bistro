Welcome to Olive & Ember

Thoughtful Cooking for Unhurried Evenings

We built Olive & Ember around a simple conviction: the best meals are never rushed. Our kitchen begins each morning with regional produce from Maharashtra’s Western Ghats, stone-milled flours, and herbs snipped just before service.

Whether you are stopping by for charred sourdough and burrata after work or gathering friends for a slow Sunday dinner, every plate is cooked to order over white-oak embers and finished with restraint.

The Olive & Ember Experience

Crafted Around Warmth, Seasonality & Care

Every detail of our dining room and kitchen is shaped to make an ordinary evening feel restorative.

01 · Ambience

Intimate Lighting & Acoustic Calm

Spaciously arranged tables, lime-washed walls, and soft acoustic playlists ensure you can actually hear the people sitting across from you.

02 · Ingredients

Small-Batch & Scratch Made

From 36-hour fermented country sourdough to hand-rolled pasta and house-infused botanical spritzes, nothing arrives from a pre-made packet.

03 · Hospitality

Attentive, Unhurried Service

We never rush a table between seatings. Whether you have dietary preferences or need a quiet corner for an anniversary, our team is happy to adapt.

Before You Book

  • Confirmation Process: Requests are logged with a pending status and reviewed by our desk before confirmation.
  • Party Size Limit: Online requests accommodate 1–12 guests. For groups of 13 or more, please reach out via our Contact page.
  • Grace Period: Held tables are kept for 15 minutes past the confirmed reservation time.
  • Closed Mondays: Please note that our dining room is closed on Mondays for bakehouse prep and farm sourcing in our sample schedule.
